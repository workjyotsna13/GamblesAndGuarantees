# Insurance in a two state world

Initial wealth w. With probability π a loss L occurs. Insurance sells coverage Q at premium rate q per unit, so the premium bill is qQ.

Final wealth:

- no loss: w - qQ
- loss: w - L - qQ + Q

An expected utility maximiser chooses Q to maximise

(1-π) u(w - qQ) + π u(w - L + (1-q)Q)

At an actuarially fair price q = π, full insurance (Q = L) is optimal for any strictly risk averse agent. When q > π, partial coverage is typically optimal. The wedge between willingness to pay and the fair price is exactly the risk premium intuition from Arrow Pratt.
