# Arrow Pratt measures

Absolute risk aversion

r_A(w) = - u''(w) / u'(w)

Relative risk aversion

r_R(w) = - w u''(w) / u'(w)

Larger r_A means a higher risk premium for a small actuarially fair gamble around w. Comparing two agents, the one with pointwise higher absolute risk aversion is more risk averse in the Arrow Pratt sense and accepts a smaller set of gambles.

For CRRA utility with parameter γ > 0,

u(w) = (w^(1-γ) - 1) / (1-γ)   for γ ≠ 1

and log utility when γ = 1. Then r_R(w) = γ everywhere.
