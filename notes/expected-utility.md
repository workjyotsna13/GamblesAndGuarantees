# Expected utility

Under the von Neumann Morgenstern axioms, preferences over lotteries admit a representation

U(g) = sum_i p_i u(a_i)

where u is a Bernoulli utility over outcomes and p_i are probabilities.

Risk attitude is read off the curvature of u:

- concave u: risk averse (prefers the expected value to the lottery)
- linear u: risk neutral
- convex u: risk loving

The certainty equivalent CE solves u(CE) = U(g). For a risk averse agent, CE lies below expected wealth.
