# Research question

A decision maker faces a random wealth lottery. Two objects sit at the center of the analysis.

1. The **certainty equivalent**: the sure wealth level that yields the same expected utility as the lottery.
2. The **risk premium**: expected wealth minus the certainty equivalent. It is what the agent would pay to remove the risk.

The project asks how the risk premium moves with the curvature of utility, and what that implies for the price of a simple insurance contract against a discrete loss.
